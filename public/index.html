Workbooks are generated on the server from the live database. Download after check-in so the attendance columns are current. Attendance can only be changed here or at the scanner — never by editing the exported file.

QR check-in scanner

ReadyChoose the event, start the camera, then scan each student’s QR pass.
Manual verification
The camera needs HTTPS or localhost. If it is unavailable, an authorised admin can verify a student with their registration ID — every check-in is recorded against the signed-in admin, and duplicates are rejected by the database.

On-spot registration

Register a student at the venue for On-spot or Both mode events. The record is stored in PostgreSQL, gets a real registration ID and QR pass, and appears in the event’s participant list and Excel export.
